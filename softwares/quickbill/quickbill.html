QuickBill

Professional billing software built for real retail workflows.

QuickBill is a Windows desktop billing and inventory management system designed for retail businesses. It combines fast billing, barcode scanning, product management, inventory, payments, professional invoices, bill history and connected customer display functionality in one system.

Overview

One system for the complete billing workflow.

QuickBill is designed around the complete retail checkout workflow — from product discovery and barcode scanning to billing, payment, transaction completion and invoice generation.

The desktop application remains the authoritative source for billing, products, inventory, payments and transaction records. Companion applications extend the workflow without replacing the desktop system.

Local-first architecture

Core billing and business data remain available locally.

Connected ecosystem

Android companion applications communicate with the desktop system.

Capabilities

Built around the retail counter.

The core features focus on speed, accuracy and a dependable day-to-day billing workflow.

Fast Billing

Add products quickly, adjust quantities and keep totals updated throughout the billing workflow.

Barcode Scanning

Supports barcode-based product workflows using compatible scanning hardware and camera-based scanning.

Product Management

Add, edit, search and manage products used throughout the billing and inventory workflow.

Inventory Management

Maintain product stock information alongside the billing workflow.

Payment Workflow

Supports cash, UPI, card and credit payment workflows through the dedicated payment interface.

Dynamic UPI QR

Generates a scannable UPI payment QR linked to the current transaction amount.

Customer Display

Connects with the QuickBill Customer Display Android application for live customer-facing billing and payment information.

Bill History

Review previous transactions with bill search and reprint workflows.

Hold & Resume Bills

Temporarily hold incomplete billing sessions and resume them when required.

Professional Invoices

Generate professional PDF bills for supported print formats, including A4 and thermal-style workflows.

Barcode Labels

Generate barcode label PDFs for products, shelves and packaging.

Configurable Settings

Application and billing configuration can be managed from the desktop settings workflow.

What changed.

The latest release focuses on the payment experience, connected customer display workflow and overall stability.

Payment workflow

Refined payment dialog with improved handling for cash, UPI, card and credit payment modes.

UPI payments

Added dynamic, amount-linked UPI QR support for payment collection.

Customer Display

Improved synchronization between QuickBill Desktop and the Android Customer Display application.

Connection handling

Improved LAN connection and automatic reconnection behavior for connected customer displays.

Transaction states

Improved payment pending and successful transaction state synchronization across the desktop and customer display.

Stability

General interface, workflow and release packaging improvements.
