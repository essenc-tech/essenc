Overview

A dedicated scanning interface for QuickBill.

The Barcode Scanner application is designed specifically around the QuickBill retail billing workflow. Instead of relying only on traditional scanning hardware, a compatible Android device can be used as a dedicated scanning interface.

The application is intended to complement QuickBill Desktop, allowing the desktop application to remain the central billing system while the Android device focuses on scanning.
