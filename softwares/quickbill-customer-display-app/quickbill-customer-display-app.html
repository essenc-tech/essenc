Customer Display

A customer-facing display for the QuickBill ecosystem.

QuickBill Customer Display is an Android companion application designed to show live billing information, payment details, QR payments and transaction status on a customer-facing device while QuickBill Desktop handles the actual billing workflow.

Overview

A dedicated customer screen for checkout.

The Customer Display application provides a second screen experience for customers during billing. It receives the current state from QuickBill Desktop and presents the information in a clean, readable interface.

QuickBill Desktop remains the authoritative billing and payment system. The Android application acts as the customer-facing presentation layer for the live transaction.

Live billing view

Displays products, quantities, pricing and totals while a bill is being created.

Payment presentation

Presents the active payment method and relevant payment information to the customer.

Capabilities

Everything customers need to see.

The display is built around the most important information a customer needs during checkout and payment.

Live Bill Display

Shows the current bill and updates as products are added, removed or changed on QuickBill Desktop.

Itemized Products

Customers can clearly see product names, quantities, rates and line amounts.

Clear Totals

Displays subtotal, tax, discount and final payable amount in a customer-readable format.

Cash Payment

Presents the active cash payment state during the checkout process.

UPI QR Payment

Displays the payment QR code and relevant UPI payment state directly on the customer screen.

Card Payment

Shows the selected card payment mode and its current transaction state.

Credit Payment

Presents the credit payment state when that payment method is selected in QuickBill.

Payment Success

Displays a dedicated success state when the desktop application completes the transaction.

Automatic Reconnection

Designed to reconnect automatically when the local connection becomes temporarily unavailable.

Automatic Screen Flow

Returns to the appropriate customer-facing state as the transaction moves through the QuickBill workflow.

Full-Screen Interface

Designed for dedicated customer-facing tablets and secondary displays.

Payment experience

Keep customers informed through payment.

UPI

Customer-facing QR payment presentation with the active transaction amount.

Cash

Clear cash payment state shown on the customer-facing screen.

Card

Displays the selected card payment mode during checkout.

Success

Shows the completed transaction state after QuickBill Desktop confirms payment.
